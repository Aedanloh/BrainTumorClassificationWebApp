Medical Encyclopedia

Anatomical & Diagnostic References

Standard neurological pathology definitions and typical magnetic resonance signs.

Glioma

Astrocytic & Glial Mass Pathologies

Location: Cerebral Hemispheres, Brainstem, Optic Tracts

Tumorous masses arising from brain glial support cells (astrocytes, oligodendrocytes, ependymal cells). Glioblastomas represent high-grade (WHO Grade IV) variations.

MRI Diagnostics

  • Poorly demarcated infiltrative margins
  • Central hypointense necrosis on T1w + contrast
  • Surrounding vasogenic edema on T2/FLAIR
Meningioma

Meningeal Membrane Pathology

Location: Sagittal Sinus, Sphenoid Wing, Cerebellar Tentorium

Slow-growing extra-axial tumors originating from arachnoid cap cells of the meningeal coverings. Represents the most common primary brain tumor.

MRI Diagnostics

  • Sharp, well-demarcated boundary edges
  • Homogeneous, intense contrast enhancement
  • Characteristic "Dural Tail" sign
Pituitary

Sellar & Endocrine Region Tumors

Location: Sella Turcica, Pituitary Fossa

Adenomateous growths in the pituitary gland. Can be secretory (prolactinoma, ACTH) or non-secretory, often compressing the optic chiasm.

MRI Diagnostics

  • Enlargement/expansion of the sella turcica
  • Coronal T1 dynamic sequences for microadenomas
  • Classic "snowman" or figure-8 shape
No Tumor

Normal Anatomical Configurations

Location: Symmetric Hemispheric Structures

Healthy brain MRI scan. Shows symmetric ventricular volumes, midline alignment, uniform gray-white matter borders, and no signal anomalies.

MRI Diagnostics

  • Intact midline falx placement
  • Normal signal intensity across sequences
  • No space-occupying lesions or mass effects
